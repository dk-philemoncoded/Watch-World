# PHILL — World Watch & Planetary Timepiece ⏱️🌍

An interactive, luxury analog wrist watch and full-UI 3D globe experience by **PHILL** that displays the accurate local time of any country, city, or town on Earth.

## ✨ Features

- **Hero Wrist Watch by PHILL**:
  - Ultra-detailed Swiss mechanical timepiece with a genuine, realistic clock tick (dead-beat mechanical step with zero jitter or overlap reversal).
  - 24-hour celestial **Sun & Moon complication disc** that rotates dynamically with the location's solar hour.
  - Magnified **Cyclops date aperture** displaying the target location's current calendar day.
  - Applied faceted diamond-cut hour markers with **Super-LumiNova luminescence** that illuminates in night and dusk themes.
  - Genuine alligator-grain stitched leather strap framing, fluted bezel, knurled crown, and sapphire crystal antireflective flare.
  - Case Finishes: **Stainless Steel**, **Royal Rose Gold**, **Obsidian DLC Black**, and **Vintage Bronze**.

- **Universal Search (Countries, Cities, & Towns)**:
  - Type any **Country** (e.g. *Nigeria*, *Japan*, *Spain*, *France*, *Brazil*, *Canada*, *Australia*), **City**, or **Town** (e.g. *Oxford*, *Ibadan*, *Kyoto*, *Cannes*, *Honolulu*).
  - Click **Find Time** or press **Enter**: the app immediately resolves the exact coordinates and timezone.

- **Full-UI Cinematic 3D Globe Animation**:
  - When you search for a place or click "Find Time":
    1. The wrist watch zooms in toward the camera.
    2. The 3D globe takes over and **fills the entire UI (100vw × 100vh)** against a cosmic starfield with telemetry HUD.
    3. The entire 3D Earth is completely visible (zero clipping or cropping).
    4. The globe spins around with planetary momentum, turns to the exact location, drops a glowing 3D beacon with radar ripples, and **zooms deep into that location**.
    5. Just when the place is fully zoomed in, the watch face transitions smoothly back into the foreground.
    6. The watch hands rapidly sweep to the local time of that place!

- **Dynamic Time-of-Day Background Theme**:
  - The website's background atmosphere dynamically morphs to reflect the local time of the searched place:
    - 🌅 **Dawn / Sunrise (05:00 - 06:59)**: Soft peach, blush rose, lavender, and sunrise glow.
    - ☀️ **Broad Daylight (07:00 - 16:59)**: Bright cerulean sky with sunbeam flare.
    - 🌇 **Golden Hour Sunset (17:00 - 19:29)**: Deep amber, burning crimson, and violet horizon.
    - 🌆 **Twilight / Dusk (19:30 - 21:29)**: Deep cobalt, electric indigo, magenta edge with early stars.
    - 🌙 **Midnight Starlit Night (21:30 - 04:59)**: Deep obsidian night with shimmering celestial starfield.

- **Web Audio API Escapement Sound**:
  - Crisp mechanical escapement ticking on each second step.
  - High-speed winding whirl during timezone change.
  - Crystal arrival lock chime.
  - Mute / unmute audio toggle.
